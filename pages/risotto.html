Risotto

A classic, creamy Parmesan risotto takes about 35 to 40 minutes to make using Arborio or Carnaroli rice.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (400g) Arborio or Carnaroli rice (do not wash)
  • 7–8 cups (1.5–1.8 litres) warm chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 cup (240ml) dry white wine
  • 1 medium yellow onion or shallot, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced or crushed
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter (divided)
  • 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Simmer the stock: In a separate saucepan, bring your chicken or vegetable stock to a gentle simmer and keep it warm on low heat.
  2. Sauté the aromatics: Heat the olive oil and 1 tablespoon of butter in a wide, heavy-bottomed pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook for 3 to 5 minutes until soft and translucent, then stir in the garlic for 30 seconds.
  3. 1. Toast the rice: Add the dry risotto rice to the pan. Stir continuously for 1 to 2 minutes so every grain is coated in butter and oil and the edges turn slightly translucent.
  4. Deglaze with wine: Pour in the dry white wine. Stir constantly until the wine is fully absorbed and evaporated by the rice.
  5. Add stock gradually: Ladle in hot stock about one cup or ladle at a time. Stir frequently and wait for the liquid to be mostly absorbed before adding the next ladle. Continue this process for about 18 to 25 minutes until the rice is creamy and al dente (tender with a slight bite).
  6. Mantecatura (finish off-heat): Remove the pan from the heat. Vigorously stir in the remaining butter and the grated Parmesan cheese until rich and glossy. Season with salt and black pepper.
  7. Rest and serve: Cover the pan with a lid or foil and let it rest for 2 to 3 minutes before serving.
