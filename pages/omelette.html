Omelette

A classic, fluffy omelette is a quick and satisfying meal that takes just a few minutes to cook.

Ingredients

  • Eggs: 2 to 3 large eggs
  • Butter & Oil: 1/2 tablespoon of unsalted butter and a small drizzle of olive oil (or 1 tablespoon of butter/oil total)
  • Seasoning: A pinch of fine sea salt and black pepper
  • ptional Fillings: Grated cheddar or mozzarella cheese, sliced ham, sautéed mushrooms, or fresh herbs

Instructions

  1. Beat the Eggs: Crack the eggs into a small bowl, add a pinch of salt and pepper, and whisk or beat with a fork until smooth and slightly frothy.
  2. Heat the Pan: Place an 8-inch non-stick skillet over medium-low heat. Add the butter and oil, letting the butter melt and foam without browning.
  3. Pour and Swirl: Pour the egg mixture into the pan and tilt it gently so the eggs coat the entire bottom surface.
  4. Cook and Gently Stir: As the edges begin to set (after about 20 to 30 seconds), use a spatula to gently push the cooked edges toward the center, tilting the pan to let the liquid egg flow into the empty space.
  5. Add Fillings: When the center is mostly set but still slightly soft and glossy, scatter your cheese or other toppings over one half of the omelette.
  6. Fold and Serve: Use your spatula to fold the other half over the filling. Slide the omelette onto a warm plate and serve immediately.
